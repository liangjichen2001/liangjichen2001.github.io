I am a Ph.D. student in Mathematics at the School of Mathematics
and Statistics, Nanjing University of Science and Technology.

My research focuses on statistical physics, anomalous diffusion,
and stochastic processes. I am particularly interested in stochastic
resetting, first-passage phenomena, and the optimization of search
strategies.

## Research Interests

- Statistical physics and stochastic dynamics
- Anomalous diffusion
- Stochastic resetting and first-passage problems
- Optimal search strategies
- Computational mathematics and numerical methods
